# 🌊 The Ocean Speaks.

The ocean covers **over 70% of our planet**, yet much of it remains unseen, misunderstood, and increasingly threatened.

Marine ecosystems are changing every day—from coral bleaching and plastic pollution to biodiversity loss and changing coastal conditions.

The information exists.

Understanding it shouldn't require being a marine scientist.

---

# 🌍 Our Vision

**LITTORA** reimagines marine monitoring through an immersive **Marine Digital Twin**.

Instead of fragmented dashboards and disconnected datasets, users explore a living digital ocean where environmental intelligence becomes visual, interactive, and accessible.

Through storytelling, visualization, and AI-assisted insights, Littora helps users better understand the relationship between ocean health, marine biodiversity, and conservation.

# 🎨 Design Philosophy

Rather than designing another analytics dashboard, Littora was crafted as an **immersive ocean experience**.

Inspired by:

🌊 Futiger Aero

🫧 Underwater light caustics

🐋 Blue Planet documentaries

💎 Glassmorphism

🌍 Interactive 3D environments

✨ Cinematic storytelling

Every interaction is designed to make users feel as though they are exploring the ocean—not simply observing data.
